# Canonical Component Identity Specification

## 1. The Principle of Canonical Identity

In DESK, an electronic component is defined by its physical product reality—independent of whether it is purchased from LCSC, DigiKey, or Mouser, or whether its CAD footprint is drawn in EasyEDA or KiCad.

### The Four Distinct Identifiers

1. **Manufacturer Part Number (MPN)**:
   The manufacturer's authoritative part code identifying exact silicon or passive specification (e.g., `TPS62840DLCR`, `CL05B104KO5NNNC`).
2. **Canonical DESK ID**:
   Deterministic internal identifier generated by DESK:
   $$\text{DESK\_ID} = \text{"comp\_"} + \text{SHA256}(\text{NormalizedMfr} + \text{":"} + \text{NormalizedMPN})[:16]$$
3. **Supplier SKU**:
   The distributor's catalog item number (e.g., LCSC `C347222`, DigiKey `296-TPS62840DLCR-ND`).
4. **EDA Asset ID**:
   The unique identifier for a CAD symbol or PCB footprint (e.g., `sym_local_TPS62840DLCR`, `fp_VSON-8`).

---

## 2. Manufacturer Normalization & Alias Mapping

Raw manufacturer names from supplier catalogs and BOM files vary wildly (e.g., `"TI"`, `"Texas Instruments"`, `"Texas Instruments Inc."`). The `ComponentIdentityResolver` normalizes these into controlled authoritative names:

- Controlled alias dictionary matching leading semiconductor vendors (TI, ST, NXP, ADI, Microchip, Infineon, onsemi, Diodes Inc, Yageo, Uni-Royal, Samsung, Murata, TDK, Espressif).
- Automated stripping of international corporate suffixes (`Inc.`, `LLC`, `Ltd.`, `GmbH`, `Co., Ltd.`, `Pte Ltd`).
- Fallback to cleaned title-cased name for unmapped manufacturers.

---

## 3. Disambiguation & Conflict Detection

When two incoming records share a matching MPN or canonical key, `ComponentIdentityResolver.detect_conflicts()` validates:
- **Package Match**: Flags differences (e.g., `VSON-8` vs. `WSON-8` or `0402` vs. `0603`).
- **Manufacturer Alignment**: Flags discrepancies between primary and secondary sources.
- **Ambiguity Policy**: If identity is ambiguous, separate records are preserved and tagged with `IdentityVerificationStatus.CONFLICT` for human engineering review.
